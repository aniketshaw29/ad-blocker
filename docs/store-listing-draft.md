## Name

Strict Ad Blocker

## Short Description

Block ads and trackers with aggressive filtering, a simple global toggle, and a 10-minute pause.

## Detailed Description

Strict Ad Blocker filters known advertising and tracking requests in Chrome and Edge. It uses aggressive global filtering with no acceptable-ads program.

Features:

- Ad and tracker request filtering
- Global on/off control
- A temporary 10-minute pause
- Local blocking statistics, once production counting is verified
- Automatic rule-update checks every six hours while the browser is running, with notifications when new rules are applied

Settings and counters stay in your browser. The extension includes no analytics or telemetry uploads. Rule downloads contact the update host; see the published privacy policy for details.

Aggressive filtering may interrupt site functionality. You can pause filtering globally when needed. Blocking effectiveness varies by site, and video ads cannot be guaranteed to disappear.
